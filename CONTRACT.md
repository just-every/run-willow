# Willow contract

Willow is the people register, leave ledger and joiner/mover/leaver process
for one employer of about 20–200 people with an established payroll provider.
Each clause is proven through public doors: the JSON door `/api/willow/*`,
the HTML form door `/app/do/*`, the pages under `/app`, access-checked
downloads, and `rer`. `tests/willow/flows.mjs` runs the built
Worker with real local D1 and R2; `tests/willow/journey.mjs` drives a real
browser at 1440 and 390 pixels from a fresh clone. Every person in fixtures
and tests is invented.

Private employee data is the primary correctness requirement. Every read of a
person goes through one projection (`src/willow/profile.ts`) that selects only
the facets the member may see (`src/willow/access.ts`); every GET door is
listed in `src/willow/doors.ts`, and WIL-03 crawls all of them.

| Clause | Observable guarantee | Proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WIL-01 | The owner sets up one employer (name, IANA time zone, country, currency) before anything else, then locations, departments, immutable working schedules and private document categories. Members are people given sign-in access through their work email; every permission is explicit and set only by the owner (managing someone grants none). Ending access ends every session; giving it again needs a fresh sign-in and restores no permission. | `regressions.mjs`, `flows.mjs`, `journey.mjs` |
| WIL-02 | A member with people.manage previews exactly what another member would receive about a person, by facet, computed by the same projection as that member's own requests; ended sign-ins and records the selected member cannot enter are refused before projection; values the previewer cannot read are reported as withheld, never shown. | `regressions.mjs`, `flows.mjs`, `journey.mjs` |
| WIL-03 | A member without the grant cannot read private contact details, compensation, confidential absence reasons, end-of-employment reasons or documents in a restricted category through any door: every JSON view, page, document download, payroll CSV, summary and its CSV, visibility preview and agent context. Managers see their reports' work details and leave dates only. Each holder does read what they are entitled to, and reads of someone else's private details, pay, reasons and documents are audited. | `flows.mjs` (crawls every door in `doors.ts` for five members) |
| WIL-04 | A person is separate from their employment periods. A joiner creates the person, an employment period with its type and payroll-provider identifier, and the first effective-dated terms (title, department, location, manager, schedule, pay). Work email and employee number are unique; a person's periods never overlap. | `flows.mjs`, `journey.mjs` |
| WIL-05 | Joiner, mover and leaver cases start from versioned templates: each task goes to HR, the manager in force on the case date, IT, payroll or the employee, due relative to that date. A new template version leaves open cases on their version. Only the task's assignee completes it; account and device tasks need evidence. A case cannot complete while a task is open or required information is missing. | `flows.mjs`, `journey.mjs` |
| WIL-06 | Private documents carry a category that decides whether the person and their manager may read them; holders of documents.private read all. Versions are immutable and numbered; bytes are checksummed and verified on every read; downloads are audited. A person acknowledges receipt of the latest version only, recorded as a receipt and labelled as not a signature. | `flows.mjs` |
| WIL-07 | A change of terms is a new complete version effective from a date: past, current and future versions all remain, the version in force is chosen by date, and a future or past version can be cancelled (kept as cancelled) but never edited. The active employment period wins on the as-of date (inclusive boundaries), otherwise the first upcoming period, otherwise former; directory, profile, manager scope and reports use that same selection. Setting pay needs pay.read. | `regressions.mjs`, `flows.mjs`, `journey.mjs` |
| WIL-08 | Leave policy versions are written by leave managers and approved by the owner before use. A leave account opens against an approved version with a proposed opening balance at a stated cutover date; nothing can be requested until a leave manager signs it off, which writes the account's opening ledger entry. Accrual is posted deterministically (accrual weeks × scheduled weekly hours, pro rata per day) and only once per day range. | `flows.mjs`, `journey.mjs` |
| WIL-09 | A leave request is calculated in hours from the working schedule in force on each day, shows the balance after it and who else in the team is away (names, dates and status only), and routes to the person's manager in force on the first day (or to leave managers). The stored routed approver can read and decide that request even before their management terms start, without gaining general person or account access. The manager decides without seeing the confidential reason; nobody decides their own request. | `regressions.mjs`, `flows.mjs`, `journey.mjs` |
| WIL-10 | The leave ledger is append-only. A request reserves its hours in the same atomic step that checks the balance, so concurrent requests cannot spend the same hours; concurrent decisions produce exactly one. Cancelling approved leave writes one reversal of its use; correcting it reverses the old use and uses the corrected dates in one step, against the balance. | `flows.mjs` |
| WIL-11 | A payroll change batch for a pay period gathers every start, end, terms change (with pay) and approved leave (in hours) effective by the period end and not yet handed off, keyed by the provider identifier; changes without one are listed as exceptions. All lines, exceptions and silent claims are calculated solely from one atomic source snapshot, revalidated in the draft transaction. Export atomically revalidates the reviewed source values and claims (including silent cancellation pairs), refusing stale drafts and older drafts without this calculation guarantee before any handoff; an unchanged draft hands each change off exactly once. A fresh draft nets pre-handoff cancellations to nothing; leave cancelled after handoff becomes a reversal line in the next batch; the provider's acknowledgement is recorded. Needs payroll.handoff and pay.read. Payroll calculation, tax and payment stay with the provider. | `regressions.mjs`, `flows.mjs`, `journey.mjs` |
| WIL-12 | A leaver records the period's end date and a private reason and opens a leaver case. External account and device tasks stay open until their assignee records evidence; while any is open the departure cannot be marked complete. The case's "end access" task deactivates the person's sign-in and clears their permissions. | `flows.mjs`, `journey.mjs` |
| WIL-13 | A rehire is a new employment period for the same person; earlier periods, terms and history are unchanged, and ended access is not reactivated or its permissions restored. | `regressions.mjs`, `flows.mjs` |
| WIL-14 | Summaries (headcount by department and location, starters and leavers, leave taken, open lifecycle work) state their filters and calculation; reports.read covers the organisation, a manager without it sees their team, others are refused. Compensation totals appear only for members holding pay.read too; the CSV matches the page. | `flows.mjs`, `journey.mjs` |
| WIL-15 | Agent doors without a model account: a joiner or mover is proposed through the JSON door, validated by the code that executes it, with missing information, the record versions it was prepared against and an explicit diff. HR approves the exact plan; execution and its receipt (with the plan's SHA-256) commit in one step, and a plan whose records changed is refused. Agent context for a person contains only what the signed-in member may read, names the withheld facets and cites the leave policy versions. | `flows.mjs` |
| WIL-16 | BambooHR move-in kit: an employee report (documented field names, CSV or XLSX), a time-off balance CSV and a documents ZIP with a manifest. The preview writes nothing and reports active, upcoming and former employment, repeated ids, missing hire dates, conflicts with existing Willow people, inactive rows without dates, balances in days, unmapped time-off types, explicit unknowns and gaps. Apply is bound to the reviewed fingerprint through Base's import kit with a journal and retained originals; documents keep their private category; balances arrive unsigned. Re-importing the same export adds nothing; undo removes an untouched import completely and is refused, naming the records, once anything was edited or built on. | `flows.mjs` |
| WIL-17 | `rer export` writes every Willow table and stored document to an owner-only archive without sessions or credentials; import restores it into an empty fresh clone, refusing corrupt or non-empty targets; a second export is identical; after normal setup, active members (including an invited member whose Willow access is active) recover ordinary email-link sign-in with the same member/person IDs and grants, HR admin authority is reconstructed from people.manage, and ended members remain denied. Source sessions and outstanding tokens fail at the destination. Records, permissions, the leave ledger and document bytes read back after those new sign-ins. | `journey.mjs` (`restore.mjs`), `restore-access.mjs` |

Disclosed limits: document uploads up to 10 MB; move-in files up to 8 MB each.
These are local admission limits, not hosted capacity claims.

Outside the delivered scope: payroll calculation, tax, payments, benefits, pension and
employer-of-record work; applicant tracking, performance reviews, expenses,
device management; jurisdiction-wide entitlement rules, public holidays and
award interpretation (owners supply reviewed policies and schedules); native
legal signing; live payroll
or HRIS connectors and live revocation of external accounts; Personio and HiBob
move-in kits.

## Optional connections and mapped handoff

| Clause | Observable guarantee | Proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WIL-18 | Email notices are off until the owner enables them. Task assignment/completion, leave decisions and exception-free payroll readiness use Base mail or the local outbox. Only currently active, authorized recipients receive fixed copy and authenticated links; no names, pay, private documents, confidential absence, titles, systems or evidence enter notices. Failed, missing and refused mail are visible independently of the committed business action. External work still requires evidence. | `phase2.mjs` |
| WIL-19 | OIDC is optional, configured only through runtime bindings and explicitly linked/relinked/unlinked by the owner using Base's fresh local email proof. Existing active Willow members retain the same IDs, person links and grants through either sign-in route; token roles/groups assign nothing. Bad configuration, provider failure or unlinking leaves email sign-in available. Connections reports refused token exchanges as unavailable even when discovery succeeds, and clears that observation after a successful token endpoint response. Secrets and transient identity state are excluded from owner archives and errors. | `phase2.mjs`, Base OIDC implementation |
| WIL-20 | Model controls are absent and calls refused without an owner-selected model and runtime connection. Checklist drafts send only authorized template IDs/revisions and controlled task structure, including for the owner; no free-form prompt, task title, system name or employee data is accepted. Models receives one server-only chat-completions request, with Bearer credentials and feature header. A valid response cites the actual source revision and covers each source task. Failed calls and invalid or uncited output remain failed proposals. A human accepts or declines the stored draft; acceptance never changes employment, leave or tasks, and a retired source needs a fresh proposal. | `phase2.mjs` |
| WIL-21 | The owner explicitly maps every generic CSV source column to a target heading and order, or selects ADP RUN Time Sheet Import's payroll-assigned identifier format with company, frequency, rate, check, department and policy earnings mappings. Missing source identifiers/mappings block previews; ADP hours cannot silently consume starts, ends, terms or reversals. The exact file is reviewed before export. Source and layout fences invalidate stale previews; export atomically freezes the reviewed bytes with the existing change claims, including silent pairs, and later mapping edits never change downloaded bytes. Payroll calculation and provider-account reconciliation remain outside Willow. | `phase2.mjs`, `regressions.mjs`, ADP primary guide pp 4–7 |

The named ADP layout follows [ADP's Time Sheet Import and RUN guide](https://support.adp.com/adp_payroll/content/hybrid/@runcomplete/doc/pdf/GTS_payroll_guide.pdf), payroll-assigned identifier layout: version row, eleven ordered columns, MM/DD/YYYY dates, positive leave hours, one frequency per file. The owner must reconcile Employee IDs, codes, active status and account setup with the provider before use. This is a file-format implementation, not a live account conformance claim.
