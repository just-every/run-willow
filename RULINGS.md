# Willow rulings

These entries retain the recorded decisions and their dates. The 28 October contract standard governs agent classes and company policy throughout the contract.

### RL-1 — Effective-dated terms and compensation

**Date:** 2026-10-21 · **Ruled by:** ct · **Clauses governed:** WIL-07.

A change of terms is a new complete version effective from a date: past, current and future versions all remain, the version in force is chosen by date, and a future or past version can be canceled (kept as canceled) but never edited. The active employment period wins on the as-of date (inclusive boundaries), otherwise the first upcoming period, otherwise former; directory, profile, manager scope and reports use that same selection. Setting pay needs pay.read.

### RL-2 — Effective-dated terms and compensation

**Date:** 2026-10-21 · **Ruled by:** ct · **Clauses governed:** WIL-07.

Schedule past and future changes, read profiles/directory/reports as of each boundary, cancel a version and inspect history; immutable terms and consistent manager scope survive.

### RL-3 — Atomic append-only leave ledger

**Date:** 2026-10-21 · **Ruled by:** ct · **Clauses governed:** WIL-10.

The leave ledger is append-only. A request reserves its hours in the same atomic step that checks the balance, so concurrent requests cannot spend the same hours; concurrent decisions produce exactly one. Canceling approved leave writes one reversal of its use; correcting it reverses the old use and uses the corrected dates in one step, against the balance.

### RL-4 — Typed custom employee fields

**Date:** 2026-10-19 · **Ruled by:** ct · **Clauses governed:** PEOPLE-001, PEOPLE-008.

Under ct's 19 October 2026 clarification, `people.manage` defines schemas. The owner can read and write every field; other members read HR/private values only with `people.private` and payroll values only with `pay.read`. Employee/self and manager visibility flags apply only to general fields, never to restricted fields. Value writes require `people.manage` plus the matching restricted-value permission. Classification and sensitivity are immutable. Every current or historical profile, preview, report, export and agent-context read rechecks current field visibility and current reader authority; a past date restores no access. Custom fields never enter the directory or organizational chart. Manager scope uses Willow’s existing current-terms-or-first-upcoming-terms relation, rechecked today rather than expanded by a historical date.

### RL-5 — Multi-row record tables and company assets

**Date:** 2026-10-19 · **Ruled by:** ct · **Clauses governed:** PEOPLE-003, PEOPLE-007, PEOPLE-006.

Under ct's 19 October 2026 clarification, built-in tables are emergency contacts, education, certifications/licenses and company assets; their columns are fixed. Emergency contacts keep Willow's private-contact rule: the person, the owner and `people.private` holders read them; the person, or a member holding both `people.manage` and `people.private`, writes them; managers never do. Exactly one in-force contact may carry the primary flag. Education, certifications and assets are general tables under PEOPLE-001: `people.manage` reads and writes them, and the person and their current direct manager read them only through HR's self/manager flags, which default on. Custom tables take PEOPLE-001's general, HR/private or payroll classification, which never changes, and its typed columns (text, exact decimal, Gregorian ISO date, boolean, select), validation and requiredness; restricted tables never gain self/manager reads. Current flags and current grants govern every current and historical read: profile, JSON, agent context, visibility preview, report, CSV and ZIP. Private-contact and restricted reads are audited.

### RL-6 — Date alerts on employee dates

**Date:** 2026-10-21 · **Ruled by:** ct · **Clauses governed:** PEOPLE-004.

HR sets alerts on any standard or custom date field (PEOPLE-001/003): visa or work-authorization expiry, probation or introductory-period end, certification/license expiry, contract end, work anniversary, I-9 reverification (US-001) and benefits eligibility dates. Each alert names the field, its lead times (such as 60, 30 and 7 days before), recipients by role (HR, the manager in force on the date, the employee, a named member) and channels (in-app, email under WIL-18, Slack/Teams under COMMS-001). Recipients are rechecked against the field's visibility each time an alert fires, so a member who cannot read a field never receives its alert and notice content never carries the restricted value. Alerts appear in a scoped upcoming-dates list, can open a FLOW-002 task with an owner and deadline (such as a probation review), fire once per lead time across scheduler restarts and stop when the date changes, the alert is retired or the person leaves.

### RL-7 — Date alerts on employee dates

**Date:** 2026-10-19 · **Ruled by:** ct · **Clauses governed:** PEOPLE-004.

Current semantics (19 October 2026, ct ruling 15:31Z): HR (`people.manage`) defines alerts on the work anniversary (the next anniversary of the employment start; Feb 29 falls on Feb 28 in other years), the employment end date (contract end), any custom date field (PEOPLE-001) and any date column of a record table (PEOPLE-003, such as certification expiry), with whole-day lead times, recipients by role (HR = every `people.manage` holder, the person's manager in force on the day the alert fires, the employee, or a named member) and channels (in Willow, and email under WIL-18 when notices are on). On each scheduler tick, in the employer's time zone and in any hour, an alert fires for a date once the day reaches the date minus a lead time and the date is today or later; only the smallest lead time already reached fires, once per alert, person, item, date value and lead time, so restarts never repeat one, a moved date starts afresh and the old date's later leads never fire. Each recipient is rechecked against the field's current visibility when it fires and again when reading an earlier alert; email has fixed text and a sign-in link and never carries the field, its value, the person or the date. An alert may open one review task per date for HR or the manager, due on the date; it is FLOW-002 work for its role holder who may read the date, and it is cancelled when the date moves, the alert is retired or the person leaves. Retired alerts, and people with no current or upcoming employment period, fire nothing. The upcoming-dates list (`/app/dates`, `GET /api/willow/upcoming-dates`) shows each alerted date from today through the alert's longest lead time, for people and fields the reader may read, with its owner (the first recipient) and due date. Until US-001 ships, I-9 reverification is a restricted HR custom date field; US-001 must expose its statutory reverification date as a PEOPLE-004 alertable field before it ships, BENEFITS-001 its eligibility dates, and COMMS-001 must add Slack/Teams as a channel with the same recheck and content rules.

### RL-16 — Group-scoped levels and bulk assignment

**Date:** 2026-10-21 · **Ruled by:** ct · **Clauses governed:** ROLE-002, ROLE-020.

The "about others" part of a level is scoped to everyone, direct reports, direct and indirect reports, or a group defined by location, department, division, employment type, legal entity or custom field. ### RL-17 — Group-scoped levels and bulk assignment

**Date:** 2026-10-21 · **Ruled by:** ct · **Clauses governed:** ROLE-002, ROLE-020.

A fresh batch-revert review shows each member's exact prior and restored assigned levels, permissions and personal write modes. It restores those levels atomically, retaining direct grants, and is available only once. A later business write by a batch member or referencing their member/person, an assignment/access change or an edit to any prior/new level definition is a dependency and blocks revert. Unrelated work, read/download/export audits and refused access-escalation attempts do not block it. The complete audited-snapshot guard is rechecked at apply, so a dependency arriving after preview cannot race the revert. Older batches lacking the captured prior assignments cannot be reverted; their current rights remain unchanged.
Scope precedence (ct, 21 October 2026): a level's scope limits only the about-others authority that level contributes; it never cuts or widens any other source. Effective access is the union of: owner status; owner-set direct grants (employer-wide, unscoped, WIL-01); each assigned level's about-others rights for the people inside that level's scope; the member's own about-themselves rights; dated direct-manager rights over their reports (WIL-03, WIL-07); the stored routed approver's read and decision of that leave request only (WIL-09); change-request step-holder reads (FLOW-004); performance summaries released to them (PERFORM-001); and the PEOPLE-002 directory projection. Rights never combine across scopes: one level's compensation view for location A plus another level's job view for location B gives neither view of the other location. Membership uses the WIL-07 terms selection on the employer-local current date (active period, else first upcoming, else former), effective dates inclusive. A transfer therefore changes access on its effective date, on the next request, with no edit or fresh sign-in, and viewing a past or future as-of date restores no access. Scope kinds: everyone; direct reports; direct and indirect reports (the in-force manager chain, self excluded); or a group over one or more of location, department, division, employment type, legal entity and a general custom-field option, OR within a dimension and AND across dimensions. An empty group is refused, and HR/private or payroll custom fields cannot define a scope. Every existing level migrates to scope everyone, and built-in Manager keeps its direct-report boundary, so migration changes no effective right. People who are out of scope and reachable only through a scoped level are absent from lists, search, counts, reports and exports. Their record doors return 404 unless another source grants a read, and then exactly that source's facets appear. Scoped edit, approval and decision authority applies only to in-scope people and is rechecked inside the write: FLOW-004 HR steps, SELF-001 HR decisions, leave-manager routing and the WIL-11 queues. Employer-wide settings and definitions need scope everyone. Before they ship, ROLE-001 (settings selection), ROLE-003 (a delegate assigns only within their own scope and never widens a scope beyond it), API-001–003 and MOBILE-001 apply these scopes. Division and legal-entity scopes need those person attributes first.

### RL-18 — Group-scoped levels and bulk assignment

**Date:** 2026-10-22 · **Ruled by:** ct · **Clauses governed:** ROLE-002, ROLE-020.

Current scope slice (22 October 2026): a level has one scope: everyone (the scope every existing level migrated to), direct reports, direct and indirect reports, or a group. A group chooses values of location, department, employment type or one general custom field (HR and payroll fields are refused), matches any chosen value within a kind and every chosen kind, and is refused when empty. Division and legal entity are person attributes of this slice: a division groups departments (`division.create`, `department.division`), so a person's division is that of the department in their terms in force and a restructuring applies on the next request; a legal entity (`entity.create`, `period.entity`) belongs to one employment period and applies to the whole period. US-002 extends this legal-entity registry with FEINs and filings and keeps one entity per employment period. Membership is resolved on each request on the employer-local date from the WIL-07 selection, with a former person judged by the terms in force on their last day; reports exclude the member, and a group includes the member when they match. A level whose scope is not everyone may hold only people.manage, people.private, pay.read, documents.private, leave.manage and reports.read, which every door checks per person; the preview refuses any other permission, which stays employer-wide. A scoped level's rights are never in the member's employer-wide permission set, so Base administrator roles, member and approver pickers, settings, templates, employer configuration, payroll, audit and every other employer-wide door stay closed to them. The record door of an out-of-scope active person is exactly the PEOPLE-002 directory projection, which every member holds; every door for data beyond it (cases, leave requests, documents, former people, private values) is 404 or withholds it. The person projection (page, JSON, agent context and visibility preview), leave requests and balances, documents and downloads, record tables, custom fields, summaries, custom reports and CSV/XLSX/ZIP exports follow the scope, and the review names each member's scoped rights and today's reach. The per-person writes that follow a scope are directory and private profile updates and leave request, withdrawal, decision, cancellation and correction: each is rechecked against fresh terms inside the write, and fails if any audited change lands before it commits. The scoped decision doors are FLOW-004 requests and HR steps (and the HR step an unavailable manager hands over), SELF-001 HR decisions and the WIL-11 exception reconciliation, HR lifecycle tasks and case completion, and the work items they feed (FLOW-002 sources show a scoped holder only in-scope items); each is rechecked against fresh terms inside the write. Every other write door (joiner, mover and leaver start, document uploads, access changes, templates, policies, payroll) still needs the employer-wide right. Editing a level's scope is a reviewed, versioned level change that applies on the next request.
