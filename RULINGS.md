### RL-4 — Typed custom employee fields

**Date:** 2026-10-19 · **Ruled by:** ct · **Clauses governed:** PEOPLE-001, PEOPLE-008, PEOPLE-008.

Under ct's 19 October 2026 clarification, `people.manage` defines schemas. The owner can read and write every field; other members read HR/private values only with `people.private` and payroll values only with `pay.read`. Employee/self and manager visibility flags apply only to general fields, never to restricted fields. Value writes require `people.manage` plus the matching restricted-value permission. Classification and sensitivity are immutable. Every current or historical profile, preview, report, export and agent-context read rechecks current field visibility and current reader authority; a past date restores no access. Custom fields never enter the directory or organizational chart. Manager scope uses Willow’s existing current-terms-or-first-upcoming-terms relation, rechecked today rather than expanded by a historical date.

### RL-5 — Multi-row record tables and company assets

**Date:** 2026-10-19 · **Ruled by:** ct · **Clauses governed:** PEOPLE-003, PEOPLE-006, PEOPLE-007, PEOPLE-007, PEOPLE-006.

Under ct's 19 October 2026 clarification, built-in tables are emergency contacts, education, certifications/licenses and company assets; their columns are fixed. Emergency contacts keep Willow's private-contact rule: the person, the owner and `people.private` holders read them; the person, or a member holding both `people.manage` and `people.private`, writes them; managers never do. Exactly one in-force contact may carry the primary flag. Education, certifications and assets are general tables under PEOPLE-001, PEOPLE-008: `people.manage` reads and writes them, and the person and their current direct manager read them only through HR's self/manager flags, which default on. Custom tables take PEOPLE-001, PEOPLE-008's general, HR/private or payroll classification, which never changes, and its typed columns (text, exact decimal, Gregorian ISO date, boolean, select), validation and requiredness; restricted tables never gain self/manager reads. Current flags and current grants govern every current and historical read: profile, JSON, agent context, visibility preview, report, CSV and ZIP. Private-contact and restricted reads are audited.

### RL-7 — Date alerts on employee dates

**Date:** 2026-10-19 · **Ruled by:** ct · **Clauses governed:** PEOPLE-004.

Current semantics (19 October 2026, ct ruling 15:31Z): HR (`people.manage`) defines alerts on the work anniversary (the next anniversary of the employment start; Feb 29 falls on Feb 28 in other years), the employment end date (contract end), any custom date field (PEOPLE-001, PEOPLE-008) and any date column of a record table (PEOPLE-003, PEOPLE-006, PEOPLE-007, such as certification expiry), with whole-day lead times, recipients by role (HR = every `people.manage` holder, the person's manager in force on the day the alert fires, the employee, or a named member) and channels (in Willow, and email under WIL-18 when notices are on). On each scheduler tick, in the employer's time zone and in any hour, an alert fires for a date once the day reaches the date minus a lead time and the date is today or later; only the smallest lead time already reached fires, once per alert, person, item, date value and lead time, so restarts never repeat one, a moved date starts afresh and the old date's later leads never fire. Each recipient is rechecked against the field's current visibility when it fires and again when reading an earlier alert; email has fixed text and a sign-in link and never carries the field, its value, the person or the date. An alert may open one review task per date for HR or the manager, due on the date; it is FLOW-002 work for its role holder who may read the date, and it is cancelled when the date moves, the alert is retired or the person leaves. Retired alerts, and people with no current or upcoming employment period, fire nothing. The upcoming-dates list (`/app/dates`, `GET /api/willow/upcoming-dates`) shows each alerted date from today through the alert's longest lead time, for people and fields the reader may read, with its owner (the first recipient) and due date. Until US-001 ships, I-9 reverification is a restricted HR custom date field; US-001 must expose its statutory reverification date as a PEOPLE-004 alertable field before it ships, BENEFITS-001 its eligibility dates, and COMMS-001 must add Slack/Teams as a channel with the same recheck and content rules.

### RL-17 — Group-scoped levels and bulk assignment

**Date:** 2026-10-21 · **Ruled by:** ct · **Clauses governed:** ROLE-002, ROLE-020, ROLE-020.

A fresh batch-revert review shows each member's exact prior and restored assigned levels, permissions and personal write modes. It restores those levels atomically, retaining direct grants, and is available only once. A later business write by a batch member or referencing their member/person, an assignment/access change or an edit to any prior/new level definition is a dependency and blocks revert. Unrelated work, read/download/export audits and refused access-escalation attempts do not block it. The complete audited-snapshot guard is rechecked at apply, so a dependency arriving after preview cannot race the revert. Older batches lacking the captured prior assignments cannot be reverted; their current rights remain unchanged.
Scope precedence (ct, 21 October 2026): a level's scope limits only the about-others authority that level contributes; it never cuts or widens any other source. Effective access is the union of: owner status; owner-set direct grants (employer-wide, unscoped, WIL-01); each assigned level's about-others rights for the people inside that level's scope; the member's own about-themselves rights; dated direct-manager rights over their reports (WIL-03, WIL-07); the stored routed approver's read and decision of that leave request only (WIL-09); change-request step-holder reads (FLOW-004); performance summaries released to them (PERFORM-001, PERFORM-002); and the PEOPLE-002 directory projection. Rights never combine across scopes: one level's compensation view for location A plus another level's job view for location B gives neither view of the other location. Membership uses the WIL-07 terms selection on the employer-local current date (active period, else first upcoming, else former), effective dates inclusive. A transfer therefore changes access on its effective date, on the next request, with no edit or fresh sign-in, and viewing a past or future as-of date restores no access. Scope kinds: everyone; direct reports; direct and indirect reports (the in-force manager chain, self excluded); or a group over one or more of location, department, division, employment type, legal entity and a general custom-field option, OR within a dimension and AND across dimensions. An empty group is refused, and HR/private or payroll custom fields cannot define a scope. Every existing level migrates to scope everyone, and built-in Manager keeps its direct-report boundary, so migration changes no effective right. People who are out of scope and reachable only through a scoped level are absent from lists, search, counts, reports and exports. Their record doors return 404 unless another source grants a read, and then exactly that source's facets appear. Scoped edit, approval and decision authority applies only to in-scope people and is rechecked inside the write: FLOW-004 HR steps, SELF-001 HR decisions, leave-manager routing and the WIL-11 queues. Employer-wide settings and definitions need scope everyone. Before they ship, ROLE-001, ROLE-016, ROLE-017, ROLE-018, ROLE-019 (settings selection), ROLE-003, ROLE-021, ROLE-022 (a delegate assigns only within their own scope and never widens a scope beyond it), API-001–003 and MOBILE-001 apply these scopes. ### RL-47 — Manager-initiated change requests through approval chains

**Date:** 2026-10-19 · **Ruled by:** ct · **Clauses governed:** FLOW-004.

Current semantics (19 October 2026, ct rulings 15:31Z): a request is typed **compensation** when it changes pay and **job** otherwise, and HR (`people.manage`) records one chain per type, appended as a new version, naming the mover workflow the approved change starts. Chain steps are roles: the subject's manager's manager in force today (a named member), any HR member, or any payroll member (`payroll.handoff` + `pay.read`). A step may apply only when the pay change is at or above a percentage (inclusive; a basis change always meets it) or only when the person's location after the change is one of a list; a request always has at least one step, and a skip-level step with no manager's manager holding active access goes to HR and says so. The requester is the person's current direct manager or an HR member, never the person themselves. A requester without `pay.read` asks for a percentage, applied to the pay in force on the effective date and rounded half up to the cent; with `pay.read` an exact amount may be given. A request sets the fields it names and never clears one. FTE is the working schedule in the dated terms; an employment-type change applies through WIL-04's mechanics in the same commit: the current period ends the day before the effective date and a new period with the new type starts on it, carrying the approved terms and payroll identifier (leave accounts stay with the ended period), and the request page says so before approval. Without `pay.read`, approvers see that pay changes with both values withheld; the requester sees only the percentage they entered. Sending back needs a comment; any edit by the requester appends a revision and restarts the chain at its first step with the chain re-resolved for the edited values. Final approval executes only while the employment period is unchanged since the revision was made. While a named manager's manager has no active access, is not employed or is on approved leave today (FLOW-002 availability), HR approves that step in their place; each open step is FLOW-002 work for its eligible approvers, and a returned request is the requester's. Attachments (up to 10 MB each, checksummed) are readable by everyone who may read the request. A subject with people.manage or the owner retains the full read but never decides or edits. A subject who holds only a chain step gets no full read. ROLE-001, ROLE-016, ROLE-017, ROLE-018, ROLE-019 adds its default-off, level-granted status-only read when no existing full read applies. COMP-001, COMP-002 adds dated band-position conditions with per-revision resolution, unchanged pay withholding and stale-band refusal. US-002 must add legal-entity chain conditions before it ships.
