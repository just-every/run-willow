Willow works leave out in hours from each person's own working schedule, keeps every balance in a ledger that is only ever added to, and sends each request to the person's manager.

## Open a leave account

On a person's page, **Open a leave account** asks for an approved leave policy, the cutover date and the opening balance in hours, with a note of where the balance comes from. The opening balance is a proposal until someone with **Manage leave policies, balances and confidential reasons** signs it off. **Post accrual** then adds the hours the policy accrues up to a date.

## Request leave

Anyone with a leave account opens **Leave**, picks the leave type, the first and the last day, and presses **Calculate**. Willow shows:

- the hours from their working schedule, day by day;
- **Balance now** and the balance after the request;
- who else in their team is away then.

They may add a reason. The reason is confidential: their manager does not see it. **Submit request** sends it on.

## Decide

The request appears under **Leave to decide** on the manager's Home and **Waiting for your decision** on Leave. The manager approves or declines it, with a note if they like, and sees the dates and hours but never the confidential reason. Nobody decides their own request.

## Change your mind

A pending request can be withdrawn. Approved leave can be cancelled or its dates corrected; Willow writes a reversing ledger entry rather than deleting anything, so the history stays. Approved leave and reversals go to payroll in hours with the next [payroll handoff](/docs/payroll).
