# Willow record rules

These rules describe record formats, calculations and the detailed native workflows behind the contract. Clause outcomes remain in CONTRACT.md.

## WIL-01 — Employer setup and explicit membership

The owner sets up one employer (name, IANA time zone, country, currency) before anything else, then locations, departments, immutable working schedules and private document categories. Members are people given sign-in access through their work email; every permission is explicit and set only by the owner (managing someone grants none). Ending access ends every session; giving it again needs a fresh sign-in and restores no permission. Owner-set direct grants are this clause's current guarantee; ROLE-001, ROLE-016, ROLE-017, ROLE-018, ROLE-019–003 add named access levels, group scoping, default self-service and delegated access administration, and must keep these guarantees (explicit grants, no rank inference, ended access restoring nothing).

## WIL-02 — Visibility preview without privilege escalation

A member with people.manage previews exactly what another member would receive about a person, by facet, computed by the same projection as that member's own requests; ended sign-ins and records the selected member cannot enter are refused before projection; values the previewer cannot read are reported as withheld, never shown.

## WIL-03 — Private records through every public door

A member without the grant cannot read private contact details, compensation, confidential absence reasons, end-of-employment reasons or documents in a restricted category through any door: every JSON view, page, document download, payroll CSV, summary and its CSV, visibility preview and agent context. Managers see their reports' work details and leave dates only. Each holder does read what they are entitled to, and reads of someone else's private details, pay, reasons and documents are audited.

## WIL-04 — People and separate employment periods

A person is separate from their employment periods. A joiner creates the person, an employment period with its type and payroll-provider identifier, and the first effective-dated terms (title, department, location, manager, schedule, pay). Work email and employee number are unique; a person's periods never overlap.

## WIL-05 — Versioned joiner, mover and leaver cases

Joiner, mover and leaver cases start from versioned templates: each task goes to HR, the manager in force on the case date, IT, payroll or the employee, due relative to that date. A new template version leaves open cases on their version. Only the task's assignee completes it; account and device tasks need evidence. A case cannot complete while a task is open or required information is missing.

## WIL-06 — Private immutable documents and receipt acknowledgments

Private documents carry a category that decides whether the person and their manager may read them; holders of documents.private read all. Versions are immutable and numbered; bytes are checksummed and verified on every read; downloads are audited. A person acknowledges receipt of the latest version only, recorded as a receipt and labeled as not a signature.

## WIL-07 — Effective-dated terms and compensation

A change of terms is a new complete version effective from a date: past, current and future versions all remain, the version in force is chosen by date, and a future or past version can be canceled (kept as canceled) but never edited. The active employment period wins on the as-of date (inclusive boundaries), otherwise the first upcoming period, otherwise former; directory, profile, manager scope and reports use that same selection. Setting pay needs pay.read.

## WIL-08 — Approved leave policies and opening balances

Leave policy versions are written by leave managers and approved by the owner before use. A leave account opens against an approved version with a proposed opening balance at a stated cutover date; nothing can be requested until a leave manager signs it off, which writes the account's opening ledger entry. Accrual is posted deterministically (accrual weeks × scheduled weekly hours, pro rata per day) and only once per day range.

## WIL-09 — Schedule-based requests and routed approval

A leave request is calculated in hours from the working schedule in force on each day, shows the balance after it and who else in the team is away (names, dates and status only), and routes to the person's manager in force on the first day (or to leave managers). The stored routed approver can read and decide that request even before their management terms start, without gaining general person or account access. The manager decides without seeing the confidential reason; nobody decides their own request.

## WIL-10 — Atomic append-only leave ledger

The leave ledger is append-only. A request reserves its hours in the same atomic step that checks the balance, so concurrent requests cannot spend the same hours; concurrent decisions produce exactly one. Canceling approved leave writes one reversal of its use; correcting it reverses the old use and uses the corrected dates in one step, against the balance.

## WIL-11 — Reviewed payroll change batches

A payroll change batch for a pay period gathers every start, end, terms change (with pay) and approved leave (in hours) effective by the period end and not yet handed off, keyed by the provider identifier; changes without one are listed as exceptions. All lines, exceptions and silent claims are calculated solely from one atomic source snapshot, revalidated in the draft transaction. Export atomically revalidates the reviewed source values and claims (including silent cancellation pairs), refusing stale drafts and older drafts without this calculation guarantee before any handoff; an unchanged draft hands each change off exactly once. A fresh draft nets pre-handoff cancellations to nothing; leave canceled after handoff becomes a reversal line in the next batch; the provider's acknowledgment is recorded. Needs payroll.handoff and pay.read. Payroll calculation, tax and payment stay with the provider.

## WIL-12 — Evidence-gated departure and native access termination

A leaver records the period's end date and a private reason and opens a leaver case. External account and device tasks stay open until their assignee records evidence; while any is open the departure cannot be marked complete. The case's "end access" task deactivates the person's sign-in and clears their permissions.

## WIL-13 — Rehire without restored authority

A rehire is a new employment period for the same person; earlier periods, terms and history are unchanged, and ended access is not reactivated or its permissions restored.

## WIL-14 — Scoped workforce summaries

Summaries (headcount by department and location, starters and leavers, leave taken, open lifecycle work) state their filters and calculation; reports.read covers the organization, a manager without it sees their team, others are refused. Compensation totals appear only for members holding pay.read too; the CSV matches the page.

## WIL-15 — Human-approved agent proposals and restricted context

Agent doors without a model account: a joiner or mover is proposed through the JSON door, validated by the code that executes it, with missing information, the record versions it was prepared against and an explicit diff. HR approves the exact plan; execution and its receipt (with the plan's SHA-256) commit in one step, and a plan whose records changed is refused. Agent context for a person contains only what the signed-in member may read, names the withheld facets and cites the leave policy versions.

## WIL-16 — Reviewed BambooHR move-in with guarded undo

BambooHR move-in kit: an employee report (documented field names, CSV or XLSX), a time-off balance CSV and a documents ZIP with a manifest. Only the signed-in owner or a valid action-bound Business grant imports; HR can still read legacy import summaries without originals. The page and clients use one Base import door, with Business disabled until the owner opts in and permanent key revocations retained. Uploads and plans are private custody; the preview changes no native people or employment records and reports active, upcoming and former employment, repeated ids, missing hire dates, conflicts with existing Willow people, inactive rows without dates, balances in days, unmapped time-off types, explicit unknowns and gaps. Apply is bound to the reviewed fingerprint through Base's single upload/plan/apply/reconcile/undo door with a journal and retained originals; owner history and undo include locally and Business-approved jobs, even with Business disabled; documents keep their private category; balances arrive unsigned. Re-importing the same export adds nothing; undo removes an untouched import completely and is refused, naming the records, once anything was edited or built on. Catch-up previews and applies changes only to untouched imported records; undoing it restores the baseline before that baseline can be undone.

## WIL-17 — Complete current-edition archive and restored sign-in

`rer export` writes every Willow table and stored document to an owner-only archive without sessions or credentials; import restores it into an empty fresh clone, refusing corrupt or non-empty targets; a second export is identical; after normal setup, active members (including an invited member whose Willow access is active) recover ordinary email-link sign-in with the same member/person IDs and grants, HR admin authority is reconstructed from people.manage, and ended members remain denied. Source sessions and outstanding tokens fail at the destination. Records, permissions, the leave ledger and document bytes read back after those new sign-ins. External connection consent is not portable: archive settings disable notices and identity consent and clear the selected model, leaving the source unchanged; the restored owner reauthorizes each connection (DATA-001). An older archive carrying enabled consent remains restorable: import forces its notices, OIDC and model settings off without changing the original backup, flags owner reauthorization and records actual per-table/column safe replacement counts in Base's import report (`importSafety`/`importRow`). Restored Business import enablement is also forced off; consumed nonces, permanent revoked keys, approval history and custody remain intact.

## WIL-18 — Optional safe business-event email

Email notices are off until the owner enables them. Task assignment/completion, leave decisions and exception-free payroll readiness use Base mail or the local outbox. Only currently active, authorized recipients receive fixed copy and authenticated links; no names, pay, private documents, confidential absence, titles, systems or evidence enter notices. Failed, missing and refused mail are visible independently of the committed business action. External work still requires evidence.

## WIL-19 — Optional OIDC with explicit identity linking

OIDC is optional, configured only through runtime bindings and explicitly linked/relinked/unlinked by the owner using Base's fresh local email proof. Existing active Willow members retain the same IDs, person links and grants through either sign-in route; token roles/groups assign nothing. Bad configuration, provider failure or unlinking leaves email sign-in available. Connections reports refused token exchanges as unavailable even when discovery succeeds, and clears that observation after a successful token endpoint response. Secrets and transient identity state are excluded from owner archives and errors.

## WIL-20 — Structural model checklist drafts under human control

Model controls are absent and calls refused without an owner-selected model and runtime connection. Checklist drafts send only authorized template IDs/revisions and controlled task structure, including for the owner; no free-form prompt, task title, system name or employee data is accepted. Models receives one server-only chat-completions request, with Bearer credentials and feature header. A valid response cites the actual source revision and covers each source task. Failed calls and invalid or uncited output remain failed proposals. A human accepts or declines the stored draft; acceptance never changes employment, leave or tasks, and a retired source needs a fresh proposal.

## WIL-21 — Frozen mapped payroll files and ADP RUN compatibility

The owner explicitly maps every generic CSV source column to a target heading and order, or selects ADP RUN Time Sheet Import's payroll-assigned identifier format with company, frequency, rate, check, department and policy earnings mappings. Missing source identifiers/mappings block previews; ADP hours cannot silently consume starts, ends, terms or reversals. The exact file is reviewed before export. Source and layout fences invalidate stale previews; export atomically freezes the reviewed bytes with the existing change claims, including silent pairs, and later mapping edits never change downloaded bytes. Payroll calculation stays with the provider; this compatibility handoff is one path within the connected payroll job in PAYROLL-001–005, and does not establish provider-account reconciliation.

## PEOPLE-001 — Typed custom employee fields

HR defines typed, validated fields with requiredness, effective dates, employee/manager visibility and restricted HR/payroll access. Imports, profiles, reports, exports and approved connector mappings use the same field definitions; retired fields retain historical meaning. Sensitive fields cannot become directory-visible through a schema edit.

Under ct's 19 October 2026 clarification, `people.manage` defines schemas. The owner can read and write every field; other members read HR/private values only with `people.private` and payroll values only with `pay.read`. Employee/self and manager visibility flags apply only to general fields, never to restricted fields. Value writes require `people.manage` plus the matching restricted-value permission. Classification and sensitivity are immutable. Every current or historical profile, preview, report, export and agent-context read rechecks current field visibility and current reader authority; a past date restores no access. Custom fields never enter the directory or organizational chart. Manager scope uses Willow’s existing current-terms-or-first-upcoming-terms relation, rechecked today rather than expanded by a historical date.

Definitions are immutable complete versions, selected by the latest `effectiveFrom` on or before `asOf`, with the highest version breaking a same-date tie. Values and corrections retain immutable dated versions and the definition version that gives them meaning. Text limits count Unicode code points. Numbers are exact decimal strings with inclusive declared bounds; dates are valid Gregorian ISO `YYYY-MM-DD` values. Required values are enforced for new native records and imports. Retirement prevents new use while retaining definitions and value history. The first incumbent path, BambooHR, accepts `custom:<key>` employee-report headers or an explicit column mapping and a custom-value effective date; validation, provenance, idempotency and strict guarded undo apply to custom values too.

An owner may explicitly include permission-checked general custom fields as optional columns in the generic payroll file. The reviewed batch freezes their values and field definitions at its period end; source/mapping changes require review again, and frozen-file reads recheck current visibility and authority. ADP RUN retains its fixed schema. Before any future connector mapping or incumbent move-in path ships, CONNECT-001 and MIGRATE-001/002 carry these same field-definition, validation, dated-history, immutable-sensitivity and current-permission obligations. This preserves the full target obligation without claiming those future providers or sources exist.

## PEOPLE-002 — Directory and navigable organizational chart

Employees browse and search a scoped directory by name, location, department and role and navigate a manager/report org chart. Current, future and former relationships use the same effective-date rules as the profile. HR previews reorganizations, handles vacant managers and rejects cycles without exposing pay or private contact details.

## PEOPLE-003 — Multi-row record tables and company assets

Employee records hold dated multi-row tables: several emergency contacts (with a primary flag), education, certifications/licenses with expiry and company assets (type, serial, assigned/returned dates, condition), plus custom tables HR defines with typed columns under the same visibility rules as PEOPLE-001, PEOPLE-008. Assets assigned to a person generate device-return tasks in leaver cases (WIL-12, IDENTITY-001) that close only with return evidence; expiring certifications create renewal work (LEARN-001). Tables are importable (MIGRATE-001/002), reportable and exportable under the reader's access level.

Under ct's 19 October 2026 clarification, built-in tables are emergency contacts, education, certifications/licenses and company assets; their columns are fixed. Emergency contacts keep Willow's private-contact rule: the person, the owner and `people.private` holders read them; the person, or a member holding both `people.manage` and `people.private`, writes them; managers never do. Exactly one in-force contact may carry the primary flag. Education, certifications and assets are general tables under PEOPLE-001, PEOPLE-008: `people.manage` reads and writes them, and the person and their current direct manager read them only through HR's self/manager flags, which default on. Custom tables take PEOPLE-001, PEOPLE-008's general, HR/private or payroll classification, which never changes, and its typed columns (text, exact decimal, Gregorian ISO date, boolean, select), validation and requiredness; restricted tables never gain self/manager reads. Current flags and current grants govern every current and historical read: profile, JSON, agent context, visibility preview, report, CSV and ZIP. Private-contact and restricted reads are audited.

A row is a complete version effective from an ISO date. The version in force on a date is the latest effective date on or before it, with the highest sequence breaking a same-date tie. A change or a removal appends a version, so earlier dates and full history remain readable. An asset's returned date may not precede its assigned date. Starting a leaver case creates one evidence-required IT task, due on the last day, for every asset whose latest version is not returned. The task closes only with evidence, a returned date and a condition, and that closure appends the asset's returned version in the same commit. A workflow upgrade never supersedes these tasks. Certification renewal is assigned work for HR, due on the expiry date. It opens for each certification version that is in force for a person with current or future employment and expires on or before 30 days after today (in the employer's time zone), including versions already expired. A renewed or removed version closes it, and the scheduler's reminders apply. Reports list one table's in-force rows for the people the reader may read it for, with a CSV whose `employeeNumber` column appears only where the reader sees employment. The records bundle adds `tables/<key>.csv` in the same layout. The BambooHR move-in reads that layout back, from a ZIP such as the bundle or from a single `<key>.csv`. It matches people by `employeeNumber` or BambooHR `id`, validates every row against the destination's table definitions (an undefined custom table is refused by name, not created) and imports with the same review, repeat and strict guarded undo as other records. Before LEARN-001 ships, its provider completions and certificates must write these certification rows and reuse this renewal work. Before MIGRATE-001/002 ship further incumbent tables, those tables must reuse these definitions, validation, dated history and current-permission reads.

## PEOPLE-004 — Date alerts on employee dates

HR sets alerts on any standard or custom date field (PEOPLE-001, PEOPLE-008/003): visa or work-authorization expiry, probation or introductory-period end, certification/license expiry, contract end, work anniversary, I-9 reverification (US-001) and benefits eligibility dates. Each alert names the field, its lead times (such as 60, 30 and 7 days before), recipients by role (HR, the manager in force on the date, the employee, a named member) and channels (in-app, email under WIL-18, Slack/Teams under COMMS-001). Recipients are rechecked against the field's visibility each time an alert fires, so a member who cannot read a field never receives its alert and notice content never carries the restricted value. Alerts appear in a scoped upcoming-dates list, can open a FLOW-002 task with an owner and deadline (such as a probation review), fire once per lead time across scheduler restarts and stop when the date changes, the alert is retired or the person leaves.

Current semantics (19 October 2026, ct ruling 15:31Z): HR (`people.manage`) defines alerts on the work anniversary (the next anniversary of the employment start; Feb 29 falls on Feb 28 in other years), the employment end date (contract end), any custom date field (PEOPLE-001, PEOPLE-008) and any date column of a record table (PEOPLE-003, PEOPLE-006, PEOPLE-007, such as certification expiry), with whole-day lead times, recipients by role (HR = every `people.manage` holder, the person's manager in force on the day the alert fires, the employee, or a named member) and channels (in Willow, and email under WIL-18 when notices are on). On each scheduler tick, in the employer's time zone and in any hour, an alert fires for a date once the day reaches the date minus a lead time and the date is today or later; only the smallest lead time already reached fires, once per alert, person, item, date value and lead time, so restarts never repeat one, a moved date starts afresh and the old date's later leads never fire. Each recipient is rechecked against the field's current visibility when it fires and again when reading an earlier alert; email has fixed text and a sign-in link and never carries the field, its value, the person or the date. An alert may open one review task per date for HR or the manager, due on the date; it is FLOW-002 work for its role holder who may read the date, and it is cancelled when the date moves, the alert is retired or the person leaves. Retired alerts, and people with no current or upcoming employment period, fire nothing. The upcoming-dates list (`/app/dates`, `GET /api/willow/upcoming-dates`) shows each alerted date from today through the alert's longest lead time, for people and fields the reader may read, with its owner (the first recipient) and due date. Until US-001 ships, I-9 reverification is a restricted HR custom date field; US-001 must expose its statutory reverification date as a PEOPLE-004 alertable field before it ships, BENEFITS-001 its eligibility dates, and COMMS-001 must add Slack/Teams as a channel with the same recheck and content rules.

## PEOPLE-005 — Any person's record as of a chosen date

A member opens a person's record and picks any past or future date to see that person as they were, or are scheduled to be, on that day: the employment period, job title, department, location, manager, working schedule and pay in force, the document versions filed by then, leave balances from the ledger up to that date and the lifecycle work open on it, each linked to the version history behind it. “Documents filed by then” and “lifecycle work open on it” use recorded timestamps in the employer’s time zone: only documents filed by the end of the selected day appear, with full version history still linked, and case/task completion is reconstructed as it stood on that day. Leave balances filter the ledger by effective date, rather than the timestamp when an entry was recorded. Record visibility always uses the reader’s current authority. The view uses the WIL-07 selection rules (inclusive boundaries, canceled versions never in force, scheduled versions shown as scheduled) and the reader's current access (WIL-03, ROLE-001, ROLE-016, ROLE-017, ROLE-018, ROLE-019): choosing a date never reveals pay, private contact details, reasons or documents the reader cannot read today, and a privileged as-of read is audited like a current one. The page, the JSON door and the agent context accept the same as-of date and return the same values.

## SELF-001 — Employee and manager self-service portal

One portal gives employees their profile, documents, balances, PTO requests, assigned tasks and pending changes; managers see permitted team requests, outstanding work and coverage. Employees submit contact, name, dependent and other permitted changes with validation. The member's access level (ROLE-001, ROLE-016, ROLE-017, ROLE-018, ROLE-019) determines immediate edits versus HR approval; immutable proposals show before/after, owner, due date, outcome and downstream reconciliation. An employee never edits pay or employment authority by changing their profile.

Current semantics (ct, 19 October 2026 16:04Z): preferred name and the existing private contact fields save immediately through the person's record. Legal-name changes and additions, corrections or removals of dependents require a separate HR decision until ROLE-001, ROLE-016, ROLE-017, ROLE-018, ROLE-019 supplies field-level immediate/edit-with-approval rules. Dependents are a built-in PEOPLE-003, PEOPLE-006, PEOPLE-007 private-contact table with fixed name, relationship and optional Gregorian date-of-birth columns; current private-contact authority (the person, owner or `people.private`, never managerial rank) governs native rows, histories, reports, exports and proposal reads. Only another member holding both `people.manage` and `people.private` decides an employee proposal; the requester and subject cannot approve their own revision. Direct self writes cannot bypass that decision.

`self.request` creates an immutable before/after proposal owned by HR, due seven calendar days after submission in the employer's time zone. Required values and typed dates are validated before recording. An approval checks the original person revision or dependent-table row history and definition version, then appends the native revision and an immutable commented decision atomically; a stale proposal must be rejected and submitted again. Rejection changes no native value. The subject sees pending requests, outcomes and downstream reconciliation from Home or Personal changes. Managers use their permitted task, leave-request and team-coverage doors and see the employee projection under current authority, without dependent details or private proposals.

Every approved revision enters the WIL-11 payroll exception queue and its next batch as a person-change exception, with an owner and due date. Payroll's metadata-only queue never includes dependent values or proposal snapshots. HR or an authorized payroll handoff member records a reference to resolve the owned follow-up, visible to the subject; this records a human handoff, not provider acknowledgment or benefits coverage. Pending approvals and unresolved handoffs are FLOW-002 work. BENEFITS-001 inherits feeding approved dependent/name revisions into its benefits exception/reconciliation queue before shipping; ROLE-001, ROLE-016, ROLE-017, ROLE-018, ROLE-019 inherits this proposal/decision/history/queue path for every field it marks edit-with-approval. Their unavailable capabilities are not claimed by SELF-001.

## SELF-002 — Company home: announcements, celebrations and shared files

Every member's home shows employer announcements (targeted to everyone or a group, scheduled, pinned, with optional read acknowledgment), upcoming team time off, new hires, and birthday and work-anniversary celebrations for people who opt in (birthdays show month and day only). Company files (handbook, benefits guides, policies) live in versioned shared folders visible to everyone or to groups and can require a signature through SIGN-002, SIGN-005. Announcements and files reach mobile (MOBILE-001) and Slack/Teams (COMMS-001) with the same targeting; nothing private (pay, birth year, absence reasons) is shown.

Current delivery semantics (19 October 2026, ct ruling 19:12Z): HR (`people.manage`) publishes immutable announcement and folder revisions and file versions. A home uses the latest publication revision, inclusive start/end dates in the employer's time zone, pinned announcements first, and current active employment's location or department; unlinked members receive everyone-only publications. Author preview is separate and does not grant audience membership. Every file version download rechecks the folder's current audience and retirement state. A read acknowledgment belongs to the member and exact current announcement version, is not a signature, and required acknowledgments enter FLOW-002 work until acknowledged or no longer deliverable.

Birthday and work-anniversary preferences default off, are independently chosen by the subject alone, and withdrawal immediately removes the celebration. HR cannot opt another person in. Birthday month/day comes from the canonical private date of birth; its year never enters the company projection. Active employees' opted-in birthdays and anniversaries of their current employment period appear from today through 30 days ahead, inclusive; February 29 is observed February 28 in other years. Public celebrations contain month/day, not age, birth year or employment-start year. New hires use the permitted directory projection for starts within 30 days before or after today. Upcoming approved time off uses the existing leave visibility and date-only projection, through 30 days ahead, without reasons or protected-case details.

Under ct's current-capability ruling, SELF-002 is complete for the company home that exists today. SIGN-002, SIGN-005 inherits signature requests for these shared-folder files; MOBILE-001 inherits announcements, files and celebrations with the same current targeting, consent and revocation; COMMS-001 inherits announcement/file delivery with the same current targeting and private-content restrictions. Each must exercise that obligation before shipping. No absent signature, native-app or Slack/Teams delivery is claimed by the current home.

## MOBILE-001 — Mobile work and accessible companion apps

Before shipping, carry SELF-002 company-home announcements, files and celebrations to the companion apps with the same current audience, self-only consent and immediate revocation checks.

The responsive portal and iOS/Android companion apps let employees update permitted details, read/sign documents, request PTO and complete tasks, and let hourly workers clock in and out and record breaks (TIME-001); managers approve requests and timesheets and see team coverage. Secure push notifications reveal no private employee data on a locked device. Session revocation applies on every device; reconnecting a saved draft revalidates permissions and source revision before submission. Keyboard, screen-reader and 390-pixel journeys meet WCAG 2.2 AA.

ROLE-002, ROLE-020 scope (21 October 2026, ct): mobile screens apply a level's scope exactly as the web doors do.

## LOCALE-001 — Employee-facing languages, starting with Spanish

Each member and candidate chooses their own display language. Willow ships English and US Spanish for every employee- and candidate-facing screen: self-service and company home (SELF-001/002), mobile apps and push notices (MOBILE-001), preboarding packets (FLOW-003), native signing screens and ESIGN consent (SIGN-001, SIGN-003, SIGN-004/002), careers pages and application forms (HIRING-002), the time clock (TIME-001), EEO self-identification (EEO-001/003), surveys (ENGAGE-001) and fixed notices and reminders (WIL-18, FLOW-002). Administrators add translations for custom field labels and options (PEOPLE-001, PEOPLE-008), announcements, task and workflow templates, signature-template cover text and survey questions; untranslated employer content shows in its original language, marked as such, and is never machine-translated silently. Official forms appear in the versions their agencies publish (such as the IRS's Spanish Form W-4(SP)); where an agency restricts a translation (USCIS permits the Spanish I-9 only in Puerto Rico), the official English form is completed with the Spanish version offered for reference. A signature records the language shown at signing. Stored values are language-independent; reports and exports translate labels only. Further languages are added from translation files without code changes.

## ROLE-001 — Named access levels with field-level rights

Access is granted through named access levels: built-in Employee (self-service), Manager, HR administrator, Payroll administrator and IT, plus custom levels HR creates. Each level sets No access / View / Edit / Edit with approval separately for "about themselves" and "about others" for personal fields and for documents by category; the job, compensation, time off, benefits, performance, EEO and training sections are ROLE-004 to ROLE-010, the approvals, reports and settings a level may use are ROLE-011 to ROLE-013, the built-in level definitions are ROLE-014 and the all-section crawl is ROLE-015. A member may hold an employee level and one or more administrative levels; their rights are the union, shown in the WIL-02 preview. Levels compile to the server-side facets every door enforces, so the WIL-03 crawl covers each level, and edit-with-approval changes enter the SELF-001 approval queue. Editing a level shows the changed rights and affected members before saving, takes effect on the next request without a fresh sign-in and is audited. Rank alone grants nothing; pay, tax/identity, EEO and medical sections are never granted to Manager by default.

Named-level administration follows WIL-01 ownership and the explicit bounded HR access.admin grant described under ROLE-003, ROLE-021, ROLE-022. Existing member grants migrate to individually named levels with the identical permission combination and retained grant provenance, with a per-person before/after report and zero widened rights. Built-in Employee and Manager add no grants beyond the existing self-service and dated direct-manager boundaries; HR, Payroll and IT start from the existing explicit bundles. Direct grants and assigned levels form a set union; replacing all permissions clears assigned levels, and ending access clears both. A level preview names each affected member and the added/removed explicit permissions, binds the owner and exact audited snapshot, and applies once; an intervening audited change requires a fresh preview. Fixed-grant changes apply on the next request; transitions into or out of Base administrator status retain WIL-01’s session revocation and fresh sign-in. These are the current partial implementation boundaries; the field-level target below remains required.

The personal self-write slice supports legal name, preferred name, personal email, phone, address, date of birth and the legacy emergency-contact field. With no explicit rule, legal name keeps SELF-001 HR approval and the other fields keep their existing immediate-edit behavior, so migration changes no effective right. For a field with explicit rules in assigned levels, their union is Edit immediately over Edit with HR approval over View (no self edits); an unspecified rule contributes no override. These modes grant no read permission and do not change job, compensation, document, EEO, review, survey or manager authority. The owner or an explicit bounded HR access administrator reviews and applies rules and assignments. The review names each affected member’s before/after modes; a change applies on the next request in the same session.

Approval-required personal changes use SELF-001’s private immutable before/after proposal, another authorized HR member’s decision, source-revision fence and downstream payroll and HR exception. Pending values never change the profile. HR approval rechecks the requester’s current active identity and approval mode; a later mode change or native revision blocks stale approval, while rejection remains possible. An immediate-write request cannot bypass approval, including by clearing omitted private fields. Members who sign in with Willow's own email link (or any future password) enroll a second factor: an authenticator app (TOTP, RFC 6238) or a passkey/WebAuthn security key, plus ten single-use recovery codes shown once and stored only as hashes. A lost factor is reset by the owner or an administrator holding the delegated reset right (ROLE-003, ROLE-021, ROLE-022); nobody resets their own factor, and each reset is audited with actor and reason, ends the member's sessions and requires enrollment at next sign-in.
