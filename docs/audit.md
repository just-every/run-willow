**Audit** records changes and reads of private details, pay, confidential reasons and documents. You need **Read audit trail** access. Names identify the member or integration and the affected record; links open records your current access allows. Removed or restricted records retain a readable record type.

Use **Person**, **Action**, **From date** and **Through date** to find work. The person filter follows the affected person through employment, lifecycle, leave and document records. Dates use your employer's time zone and include both selected days. **Older events** and **Newer events** preserve the filters and browse all retained events, including activity beyond the first page. Read events remain available without hiding business changes: filter to the action you need.

Agents can use the same filters on `GET /api/willow/audit`: `person`, `action`, `from`, `to`, and sequence cursors `before` or `after` (one direction at a time). Results remain newest-first event arrays. To continue backwards, pass the last result's `seq` as `before`; continue until the response is empty. API action identifiers remain stable; people see readable action descriptions on the page.
